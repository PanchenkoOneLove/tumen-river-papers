# Tumen River publications

Studies of the Tumen River and its waters in Peter the Great Bay by the V.I. Il’ichev Pacific Oceanological Institute FEB RAS (Vladivostok): river water transport, ocean models and remote sensing.

Companion list for the talk at the **5th Russia–China Marine Science Symposium**, Vladivostok, 23–25 November 2026.
